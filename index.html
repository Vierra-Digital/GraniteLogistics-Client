The platform

Everything between the order and the doorstep.

One workspace for the people moving freight and the customers waiting on it.

Order ingest

Your store POSTs an order and it lands in the operator's queue. Nothing to key in. Manual entry and CSV import are there for the exceptions.

Labels & manifests

Code 128 labels as print-ready PDFs, carrier manifests as CSV, batched by carrier and assigned to a dock lane.

Pre-sort & staging

Sort outbound volume by ZIP to bypass initial hub handling, then consolidate into load-ready units before handoff.

Chain of custody

Every handoff timestamped end to end, with condition photos captured at pickup and at delivery so damage disputes are settled with evidence.

Customer tracking

Your customer follows their own parcel and can get email or push updates as the status changes. The public page shows status, dates and destination city — never the recipient's name, address or contents.

Roles & access

Administrator, Runner, Driver and Viewer, granted per person and revocable, with an audit trail of who changed whose access.

API-first

One POST and the parcel exists.

Send one order or a batch. Every order is written and then read back to confirm it stored, because a webhook that is told "created" for an order we dropped is a shipment nobody knows about.

A partial batch returns 503 and names what stored, so your retry only resends what is actually missing.

Ask for a key at ken@usegl.com.

POST /api/orders
curl -X POST https://usegl.com/api/orders \
  -H "x-api-key: $GL_KEY" \

How a shipment moves

Seven stages, each one stamped.

  1. 1Order receivedBy API, by CSV, or keyed in.
  2. 2Intake & labelCondition photo, Code 128 label.
  3. 3Picked upCollected from the store.
  4. 4Staged at dockPre-sorted, batched, lane assigned.
  5. 5In transitManifested to the carrier.
  6. 6Out for deliveryOn the final vehicle.
  7. 7DeliveredPhoto on handoff.
